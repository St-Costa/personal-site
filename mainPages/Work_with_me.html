How I add value

I move fast from idea to evidence. I like building lots of MVPs to put business and market hypotheses to the test, letting data guide decisions in uncertain situations. I've done this both on my own projects and as a consultant. In my AI-driven business process improvement (BPI) work, for instance, we quickly tested implementations and compared their ROI to decide where to invest. My projects are proof of this: each one was born from a real need and a hypothesis worth testing.

I add the most value with my versatility: my academic background makes me technical, while my passion for sales and marketing lets me speak the language of the business side.

In practice, this makes me a translator between two worlds: someone who can bridge technical teams and commercial goals, and keep both moving in the same direction.
